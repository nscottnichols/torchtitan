## Method

Exponential LR sweep following [Smith 2015](https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.01186).
At each step, the LR is multiplied by `mult = (max_lr / init_lr)^(1/N)` = 1.148.
Loss is tracked with EMA smoothing (beta=0.98, bias-corrected).

The **optimal LR** is estimated as the blow-up point divided by 10,
or equivalently the LR at the steepest descent phase.
