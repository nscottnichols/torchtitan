## 80B LR Finder Results

First empirical 80B LR finder — previously extrapolated only.

| Optimizer | Suggested LR | Blow-up | NaN Count | Status |
|-----------|-------------|---------|-----------|--------|
| **AdamW** | **1.13e-5** | 1.13e-4 | 0/100 | Clean sweep |
| **Muon** | N/A | NaN at step 7 | 93+/100 | Broken (bf16 overflow) |
| **SophiaG** | N/A | NaN at step 7 | 93+/100 | Broken (bf16 overflow) |

### Muon/SophiaG bf16 Overflow

Both Muon and SophiaG produce NaN regardless of LR at 80B (dim=9216):
- **Muon**: Newton-Schulz iteration `A @ A` (9216×9216 matmul) overflows bf16
- **SophiaG**: Hessian estimate `grad * grad` overflows bf16

Confirmed LR-independent: NaN occurs even at LR=1e-8.

fp32 Newton-Schulz delays Muon NaN to step 16 but is 6x slower — not viable.

The overflow is **model-size-specific**: 20B (dim=5120) works fine for both
optimizers (0 NaN for Muon, 7-9 NaN for SophiaG at high LR only).

All 20B GAS runs completed with 0 NaN. Suggested LRs not captured in output
(SSH pipe buffering).

### GAS Findings

1. **Optimal LR is relatively stable across GBS** for 2B AdamW — ranges from
   4.9e-4 to 9.0e-4 across 16x GBS range (96 to 384). No strong linear or
   sqrt scaling observed at this scale.
2. **No NaN at any GBS** — the blow-up is a clean divergence, not numerical
   overflow.
3. At production scale (GBS=6144 on 512 nodes), the optimal LR may shift
   further — these 2-node results provide a lower bound.
