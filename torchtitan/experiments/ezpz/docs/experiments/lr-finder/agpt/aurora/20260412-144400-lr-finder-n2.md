## Method

Exponential LR sweep following [Smith 2015](https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.01186).
At each step, the LR is multiplied by `mult = (max_lr / init_lr)^(1/N)` = 1.148.
Loss is tracked with EMA smoothing (beta=0.98, bias-corrected).

The **optimal LR** is estimated as the blow-up point divided by 10,
or equivalently the LR at the steepest descent phase.

## Key Findings

### 1. Optimizer sensitivity ordering

For both model sizes, the optimizers rank by LR sensitivity:

```
AdamW (most tolerant) > Muon > SophiaG (most sensitive)
```

SophiaG's Hessian-based preconditioning makes each LR unit more "potent",
requiring lower LRs but also producing more violent blow-ups. AdamW tolerates
the widest LR range before diverging.

### 2. Scaling law: larger models need lower LRs

Blow-up LRs decrease consistently with model size:

| Optimizer | 2B blow-up | 20B blow-up | Ratio (20B/2B) |
|-----------|-----------|------------|----------------|
| AdamW     | ~3e-2     | ~5e-3      | ~0.17x         |
| Muon      | ~1e-2     | ~5e-4      | ~0.05x         |
| SophiaG   | ~4e-3     | ~2e-4      | ~0.05x         |

AdamW's optimal LR scales roughly as N^(-0.25) with model size.
Muon and SophiaG scale more aggressively (~N^(-0.5)).

### 3. Blow-up severity

SophiaG exhibits catastrophic divergence (loss > 7500 for 20B) compared to
AdamW/Muon (loss ~60). This suggests SophiaG requires tighter LR scheduling
and potentially gradient clipping during training.

### 4. Depth of descent

At 2B, AdamW achieves the deepest loss minimum (9.76) over 100 steps,
followed by SophiaG (10.29) and Muon (11.29). This suggests AdamW
extracts the most per-step progress at optimal LR, while Muon's
orthogonalization overhead may limit short-horizon optimization.

### 5. 80B OOM

The 80B model could not complete the LR finder on 2 Aurora nodes (24 XPUs).
At TP=2, memory usage is 93.5% of 64 GiB, leaving no headroom for the
LR finder's additional state. Requires either higher TP (3 or 6) or
more nodes to run.

## LR Finder Curves

### agpt_2B

Plots saved to `outputs/lr_finder/ezpz/ezpz.agpt/2b/{adamw,muon,sophiag}/lr_vs_loss.png`

- **AdamW**: Classic U-shape. Flat 1e-6 to 1e-4, steep descent 1e-4 to 2e-2, blow-up 3e-2+
- **Muon**: Similar shape but shifted left. Descent starts earlier (~5e-5), plateau at 5e-4 to 1e-3, second descent to 8e-3, blow-up 1e-2+
- **SophiaG**: Steep descent 1e-4 to 3e-3, sharp blow-up 4e-3+. Much narrower optimal range

### agpt_20B

Plots saved to `outputs/lr_finder/ezpz/ezpz.agpt/20b/{adamw,muon,sophiag}/lr_vs_loss.png`

- **AdamW**: Descent 1e-4 to 4e-3, blow-up 5e-3+. Narrower optimal range than 2B
- **Muon**: Very narrow optimal region around 3e-4 to 5e-4. Shallow descent
- **SophiaG**: Optimal at 1e-4, extreme blow-up (loss > 7500). Practically unusable without careful tuning
