## Compile vs No-Compile (moe_500m)

| Compile | TPS    | TFLOPS | MFU   | Memory |
|---------|--------|--------|-------|--------|
| off     | **11,200** | **25.22** | **8.46%** | 8.44GiB |
| on      | 8,316  | 18.70  | 6.27% | 3.78GiB |

**Compile is 35% slower for MoE** due to dynamic routing causing recompilations.
Don't use torch.compile for MoE models.

## Key Findings

1. **Don't compile MoE models** — dynamic expert routing causes recompilations
2. **MFU plateaus around 11%** for 2b-4b models, drops for 7b+ due to AC=none overhead
3. **7b and 10b require AC=none** — full AC fails with CheckpointError from non-deterministic routing
4. **Memory scales linearly** with model size: 8 GiB (500m) → 55 GiB (10b)
5. **10b at 86% memory** — close to the limit, periodic GC stalls visible
