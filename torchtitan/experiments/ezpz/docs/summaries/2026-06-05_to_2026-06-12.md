## 10. Operational fixes

- **`os._exit(0)` end-of-main** in `ezpz/train.py`: terminates the chronic post-`Process group destroyed` hang where mpiexec waits on wedged C++ signal-handler threads on most ranks. Root-caused via `/proc/<pid>/wchan` inspection (one thread in `__do_sys_pause`, non-daemon → Python can't shut down).
- **SIGKILL `resource_tracker` daemon** before `os._exit` to suppress the per-semaphore "leaked semaphore" cosmetic warnings the daemon emits on parent disappearance.
